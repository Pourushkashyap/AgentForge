Build a website for a dog grooming and daycare studio called Happy Paws with Home, Services, Gallery, Booking and Contact pages. Use a soft sky blue and white theme with a warm yellow accent. The Services page lists grooming and daycare services, each with a name, short description, price and duration in minutes. The Gallery page shows 8 photos of happy groomed dogs and the studio. Visitors can book an appointment by choosing a service, a date and time, and entering their name, phone and email. Visitors can also send a contact message with their name, email and message.
